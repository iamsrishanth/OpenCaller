# Live voicemail: Samsung Direct voicemail and Apple Live Voicemail

This short comparison summarizes the vendors’ published SIM-call behavior. It does not imply that OpenCaller uses carrier voicemail.

| Caller and recipient question | Samsung Galaxy Direct voicemail | Apple iPhone Live Voicemail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Caller audio and flow** | The recipient can send a ringing call directly to voicemail or configure it to go there after a selected ring time. Samsung says the greeting begins and the caller can leave a message; it does not specify the exact greeting audio or whether a tone plays. [Samsung Direct voicemail guide](https://www.samsung.com/us/support/answer/ANS10012974/) | The caller is sent to voicemail; Apple supports a default or custom greeting, but the guide does not specify its exact audio or a recording tone. The caller and recipient cannot hear each other unless the recipient answers. [Set up Voicemail on iPhone](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/set-up-voicemail-iph3c99490e/ios) |
| **What the recipient sees while recording** | Samsung documents transcript assist while the caller records, including visibility while the phone is locked. Its guide explains opening the voicemail from Recents to view the recording and transcript. [Samsung Direct voicemail guide](https://www.samsung.com/us/support/answer/ANS10012974/) | A real-time transcript appears as the caller speaks. The recipient does not hear the caller unless they answer. Apple’s guide does not say that transcript text is shown on the lock screen. [Set up Voicemail on iPhone](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/set-up-voicemail-iph3c99490e/ios) |
| **Picking up mid-message** | The incoming-call screen offers an option to answer while the caller is recording. Samsung does not describe whether the partial recording is retained after pickup or the exact media handoff. [Samsung Direct voicemail guide](https://www.samsung.com/us/support/answer/ANS10012974/) | The recipient can accept the call while the voicemail is being left. The call screen also has a Stop action to stop voicemail without answering or blocking the caller. Apple does not describe whether a partial message is retained after pickup. [Set up Voicemail on iPhone](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/set-up-voicemail-iph3c99490e/ios) |
| **Saved-message inbox** | When the caller ends the call after leaving a message, Recents labels the item as voicemail; opening it shows the recording and transcript. [Samsung Direct voicemail guide](https://www.samsung.com/us/support/answer/ANS10012974/) | Voicemail appears in the Phone app’s Voicemail view or in the Recents call list. The recipient can play, read, share, or delete it. Messages stored in iCloud can sync across devices; messages not stored there may be erased by the carrier. [Check your voicemail on iPhone](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/check-voicemail-iph003dae603/ios), [Set up Voicemail on iPhone](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/set-up-voicemail-iph3c99490e/ios) |
| **When the handset is offline** | Samsung says Direct voicemail works only while the phone is on and connected to the mobile network. Its guide does not state where a call goes when those conditions are not met, so carrier fallback cannot be assumed from this documentation. It also warns that low internal storage may prevent the feature from working. [Samsung Direct voicemail guide](https://www.samsung.com/us/support/answer/ANS10012974/) | If the iPhone is off or outside carrier range, the call goes to carrier voicemail if available. [Set up Voicemail on iPhone](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/set-up-voicemail-iph3c99490e/ios) |

## Availability and limits

- Samsung documents Direct voicemail for Galaxy phones running Android 16 with One UI 8.5 or later; availability can vary by model, software, and carrier. The feature does not work for video calls. [Samsung Direct voicemail guide](https://www.samsung.com/us/support/answer/ANS10012974/)
- Apple says Live Voicemail availability depends on carrier, country or region, and language. [Set up Voicemail on iPhone](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/set-up-voicemail-iph3c99490e/ios)

## OpenCaller verification status

The Samsung and Apple comparison is based on official support documentation; no Galaxy or iPhone was available to verify the vendors’ behavior on a device. Direct SIM carrier voicemail is not enabled in OpenCaller because no carrier capability has been verified.


On an Android 17 / API 37 x86_64 emulator running OpenCaller 0.1.5 (version code 1006), a controlled Baresip caller registered as extension 1002 reached the app’s SIP extension 1001 over TLS with SRTP. The incoming screen offered **Send to voicemail** for that call. During the caller’s audio stream, the screen showed an interim transcript. Tapping **Answer** changed the screen to **Call in progress**; Asterisk showed the existing caller and app channels both up, with no replacement call. The app saved one 24-second voicemail while that call remained connected. Its transcript was “Everybody blue” in that run. Sending a second distinct caller-side test phrase after Answer left the saved WAV size and transcript unchanged. A separate test triggered **Answer** and caller hangup together; one item was saved and the call screen returned to **Ready for internet calls**. The emulator inbox played and sought a message, showed its transcript, and linked it to the matching recent call, which displayed **Voicemail · listened**. These are emulator results; recipient-to-caller speech was not verified because the Baresip host had no working ALSA playback device.

The physical Moto G57 Power (Android 17 / API 37) ran the same 0.1.5 / 1006 build. After granting Android's `ACCESS_LOCAL_NETWORK` permission, it registered to Zeus using SIP TLS and SRTP. A controlled incoming call displayed **Send to voicemail** and **Leaving a voicemail**. Tapping **Answer** returned to the active-call screen on the existing Baresip call. The phone finalized one 12.733-second WAV, playback started, seeking reached 6 seconds of 12, and the linked recent call showed **Voicemail · listened**. That saved item had no transcript. The saved duration is consistent with the greeting and initial caller audio ending before the second test phrase, but its content was not independently transcribed; live recognition and recipient microphone-source behavior remain unverified on the Moto. Revoking LAN permission for a UI check caused the Voicemail **Sync** action to show the Android access prompt; Allow restored the permission, after which sync still failed because the phone could not reach Zeus.

For a physical offline test, Wi-Fi and mobile data were disabled before the controlled caller rang extension 1001. Asterisk saved exactly one new `msg0002.wav` (191,724 bytes) in its voicemail inbox. After reconnecting Wi-Fi, the Moto could not reach Zeus at `192.168.0.14`; the app line remained unregistered and voicemail sync showed an error. Server-side offline capture is verified, while phone-side sync, exactly-once appearance after reconnection, and playback of that server message remain pending a working phone-to-server LAN path. The phone's **Updates** page and same-source in-place upgrade preservation were verified separately. No Galaxy or iPhone was available to test vendor behavior on hardware.
