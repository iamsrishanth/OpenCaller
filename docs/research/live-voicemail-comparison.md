# Live voicemail: Samsung Direct voicemail and Apple Live Voicemail

This comparison summarizes vendor support documentation. It describes SIM-call voicemail behavior, not OpenCaller behavior.

| Caller and recipient question | Samsung Galaxy Direct voicemail | Apple iPhone Live Voicemail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Caller audio and flow** | The recipient can send a ringing call directly to voicemail or configure it to go there after a selected ring time. Samsung says the voicemail greeting begins and the caller can leave a message. Its support pages do not specify the exact greeting audio, whether a recording tone plays, or whether the recipient can hear the caller live. [Samsung Direct voicemail guide](https://www.samsung.com/us/support/answer/ANS10012974/) | When a call is sent to voicemail, the caller leaves a voicemail while the recipient sees a live transcript. Apple documents default or custom greetings, but does not specify the exact prompt or tone heard by the caller. Apple says neither side can hear the other unless the recipient answers. [Set up Voicemail on iPhone](https://support.apple.com/en-euro/guide/iphone/iph3c99490e/27/ios/27) |
| **What the recipient sees while recording** | Samsung says the transcript can be visible even while the phone is locked. Its guide also documents opening the voicemail item from Recents to inspect the recorded message and transcript after the caller finishes. [Samsung Direct voicemail guide](https://www.samsung.com/us/support/answer/ANS10012974/) | A real-time transcription appears as the caller leaves the message. The recipient does not hear the caller unless they choose to answer. Apple does not say that the live transcript is shown on the lock screen. [Set up Voicemail on iPhone](https://support.apple.com/en-euro/guide/iphone/iph3c99490e/27/ios/27) |
| **Picking up mid-message** | The incoming-call screen offers an option to answer while the caller is recording. Samsung does not describe the exact media handoff or whether the partial recording is retained after pickup. [Samsung Direct voicemail guide](https://www.samsung.com/us/support/answer/ANS10012974/) | The recipient can pick up while the caller is leaving voicemail. Apple also documents a Stop action that ends the incoming voicemail without answering or blocking the call. [Set up Voicemail on iPhone](https://support.apple.com/en-euro/guide/iphone/iph3c99490e/27/ios/27) |
| **Saved-message inbox** | If the caller finishes and hangs up, Recents labels the entry as a voicemail rather than a missed call. Opening that entry leads to the voicemail message and transcript. [Samsung Direct voicemail guide](https://www.samsung.com/us/support/answer/ANS10012974/) | Messages appear in the Phone app’s Voicemail view and can be played, shared, or deleted. Apple says Live Voicemail and carrier voicemail can sync through iCloud; messages not stored in iCloud can be erased by the carrier. [Check your voicemail on iPhone](https://support.apple.com/en-ca/guide/iphone/-iph003dae603/ios), [iCloud voicemail settings](https://support.apple.com/en-euro/guide/iphone/iph3c99490e/27/ios/27) |
| **When the handset is offline** | Samsung says Direct voicemail works only while the phone is on and connected to the mobile network. The cited guide does not specify where a call goes when those conditions are not met, so carrier-voicemail fallback should not be assumed from this documentation. It also warns that low internal storage may prevent the feature from working. [Samsung Direct voicemail guide](https://www.samsung.com/us/support/answer/ANS10012974/) | If the iPhone is off or outside carrier coverage, the call goes to carrier voicemail if that service is available. [Set up Voicemail on iPhone](https://support.apple.com/en-euro/guide/iphone/iph3c99490e/27/ios/27) |

## Availability and limits

- Samsung documents Direct voicemail for Galaxy phones running Android 16 with One UI 8.5 or later; availability can vary by model, software, and carrier. The feature does not work for video calls. [Samsung Direct voicemail guide](https://www.samsung.com/us/support/answer/ANS10012974/)
- Apple says Live Voicemail availability depends on carrier, country or region, and language. [Set up Voicemail on iPhone](https://support.apple.com/en-euro/guide/iphone/iph3c99490e/27/ios/27)

## OpenCaller verification status

This is a documentation-only comparison. OpenCaller is still a baseline scaffold: no voicemail greeting, live transcription, mid-message pickup, saved-message inbox, or offline delivery behavior has been verified on a handset or SIP endpoint. No Galaxy or iPhone behavior was tested as part of this review. These vendor descriptions are not evidence that OpenCaller currently implements the same behavior.
